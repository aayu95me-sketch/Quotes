# Research Prompts for Gemini / Claude chat (ready to paste)

Why this exists: Claude Code here can't open Instagram, Meta's help pages or tracking tools. Gemini (with Google Search) can. The Zapier-Gemini connection works (tested 2026-10-20) but the free API key hit its quota on search-grounded prompts. These prompts can be run in the **Gemini app** (free) or **Claude chat** with web search, and the answers pasted back into the session or saved in `research/answers/`.

Rules to include every time: *"Use web search. Cite a URL for every claim. Never invent handles, follower counts or statistics. Mark anything you can't verify as 'unverified'. Prefer 2026 sources and official pages."*

## P1: English competitor map (Page B)
Research English-language faceless Instagram pages (text/graphic carousels or static posts) in self-improvement, discipline, habits, psychology-facts and stoicism, aimed at US/UK/CA/AU audiences. List up to 15 real accounts: handle, approximate follower count (with date), format, posting cadence, monetisation (affiliate, sponsors, products, newsletter), what is distinctive. Then: which sub-angles are saturated, which underserved, which slide-1 hook patterns are most common. Markdown table plus short analysis.

## P2: Indian competitor map (Page A)
Research Indian Instagram pages (faceless text/carousel pages and small creators) in discipline, study motivation, career advice, psychology facts and self-improvement, in Hinglish or English, for Indians 18-30. List up to 15 real accounts: handle, approximate followers (with date), language, format, cadence, monetisation. Then: does Hinglish or English perform better (evidence only), saturated vs underserved topics, typical sponsorship rates in INR for 10k / 50k / 100k-follower pages if any evidence exists.

## P3: Official platform policy (India)
From official Meta/Instagram/Facebook sources as of 2026: (1) originality / unoriginal-content rules and what "meaningful enhancement" means for template-based text pages; (2) whether AI-written text rendered into images needs the "AI info" label; (3) Facebook Content Monetization eligibility in India, photo and text post eligibility, payout to Indian bank accounts, thresholds; (4) Instagram monetisation tools in India: Gifts, Subscriptions, Stars, any bonus; (5) Broadcast Channels availability and requirements; (6) Instagram Graph API: can creator/business accounts attach trending audio via the Audio API, does Instagram Login vs Facebook Login matter, is scheduled publishing supported, current API version, is Meta app review needed to publish to my own accounts.

## P4: Getting paid from India
For an Indian individual creator with ₹0 budget: (1) which digital-product platforms pay out to Indian bank accounts in 2026 (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, Stripe, SuperProfile, Instamojo, Razorpay Payment Pages, Topmate, Graphy), fees, and whether international buyers can pay; (2) affiliate programs usable from India for self-improvement/productivity audiences (Amazon Associates India, Cuelinks, Audible, recurring SaaS programs): commissions, payout minimums, bio-link rules; (3) free link-in-bio and free email-list tools; (4) current SEBI finfluencer rules for unregistered individuals, with circular names and dates; (5) whether foreign-currency income from Meta needs an LUT / GST registration, and TDS considerations, from CBIC / Income Tax Department sources.

## P5: Trend sourcing
How can a creator find, each week, (a) trending Instagram audio usable on creator/business accounts, and (b) rising topics in self-improvement and psychology, using only free tools? Name specific free tools and their limits.
