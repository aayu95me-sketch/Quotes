## Page A: India, young adults 18–30 (Hinglish + English)

**Promise:** "Practical discipline, career and mind tips for ambitious young Indians, in language you actually speak."

**Audience:** students, job-seekers, early-career professionals in India. 25–34 is Instagram India's biggest group, and ~69% of users are men [Soft], so the tone should work for all genders but expect a male-skewed audience.

**Content pillars (rotate):**
1. **Discipline & habits:** concrete routines, "rules", mistakes to stop.
2. **Career & skills:** first job, interviews, salary talk, skills worth learning (no stock/finance tips).
3. **Mind & psychology:** why we procrastinate, focus, social anxiety basics (no diagnosis).
4. **Money basics (literacy only):** budgeting, avoiding scams, emergency fund. **No** securities, crypto, returns or broker links (SEBI risk, doc 02).
5. **Relatable Indian life:** exam pressure, family expectations, college-to-job reality. This is the shareable pillar.

**Voice:** direct, warm, a little witty. Hinglish in Roman script on the caption and hook slide; keep slide body in simple English or Hinglish, tested against each other (pilot variable). No cringe slang, no fake guru tone.

**Name ideas (check availability):** Kaam Ki Baatein · The Desi Discipline · Seedha Sa Sach · Level Up Bharat · Roz Ka Rule

**Visual system:** high-contrast, one accent colour (deep saffron or electric blue on near-black), a bold sans font with Devanagari-safe fallback if Hindi script is ever used, generous margins, big first-slide type, consistent slide number/progress bar.

**Monetisation plan (all ₹0 to start):**
- Instagram **Broadcast Channel** or a **WhatsApp Channel** from day 1, as the owned audience (free) [My judgement, verify availability].
- Free lead magnet: a one-page checklist PDF (made by Claude), to grow the list.
- Affiliates once about 1k+ engaged followers: Amazon India books/tools, Cuelinks merchants (education, productivity). Disclose every link.
- Sponsors from about 10k followers: Indian ed-tech, productivity and student-brand deals. Keep a rate card.
- Own digital product later: e.g. "30-day Discipline Planner" PDF, sold in ₹ through a platform with Indian payouts.

## Page B: US/UK/CA/AU English, adults 22–40

**Promise:** "Clear, honest psychology for building discipline and a calmer mind."

**Audience:** English-speaking young professionals interested in self-improvement. Crowded space, so the angle must be sharp: **evidence-flavoured explanations with an actionable rule**, not vague quotes.

**Content pillars:**
1. **Habit & discipline mechanics** (why willpower fails, friction, cues).
2. **Psychology explained** (cognitive biases, attention, motivation), simplified and accurate, no overclaiming.
3. **Relationships & communication** (boundaries, difficult conversations, scripts).
4. **Work & focus** (deep work, burnout signs, boundaries at work).
5. **Contrarian corrections** ("Stop doing X, do Y"). These are the comment drivers.

**Voice:** calm, precise, slightly dry. US spelling. Cite concepts by name, not made-up studies. **Never fabricate research or statistics.** If unsure, say "research suggests", or skip.

**Name ideas (check availability):** The Calm Method · Quiet Discipline · Mind Mechanics · Small Rules Daily · Plain Psych

**Visual system:** dark charcoal or warm off-white background, one muted accent, a serif headline font for contrast, minimal icons, no stock people.

**Monetisation plan:**
- Email list (free tier of a free email service) with a free checklist as the lead magnet.
- Global affiliates: books (Amazon, if the owner can use Amazon Associates for the relevant store), recurring SaaS (focus/notes/habit apps), disclosed.
- Sponsors in dollars from about 10k+ followers (apps, books, courses).
- Digital product (templates/planner) later; confirm the India-friendly payout platform first.

## Shared rules for both pages

1. **Originality:** every line is freshly written by Claude. No lifted quotes from living authors. Quotes from public-domain figures only when they genuinely fit, and attributed.
2. **No fabricated facts:** no invented studies, stats or names. Anything factual must be something I'm confident in, else it is rephrased as an opinion.
3. **Carousel anatomy (7–10 slides):** Slide 1 hook (≤12 words) · slides 2–8 one idea each · last slide = save/share prompt + follow CTA. Caption: 2–4 lines, 3–5 niche hashtags.
4. **Static Reels:** 1 image with a single punchy line (or a 3-image slideshow), 7–12 seconds, trending audio chosen from what's available to the account, volume low so the text leads.
5. **Cadence (pilot):** 1 carousel per day + 3 static Reels per week per page. Owner approves a **weekly batch** (7 days at a time) to keep effort small.
6. **AI disclosure:** apply Instagram's AI label if the platform requires it for this type of content, at upload.
7. **Money pages:** fixed footer line, "Educational only, not financial advice."
