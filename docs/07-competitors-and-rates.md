# Competitors, Rates and Language (relay runs 2003-2004)

Fetched 2026-10-20 via `research/answers/2003-*` and `2004-*`. **Honest limit:** web search returns blog listicles, not live Instagram data. Follower counts below are not verified, and I will not invent any. The real numbers need the owner's phone or a paid tracker.

## What the research could and couldn't establish
- **Format/niche evidence (English, global):** the same pattern from many faceless-page guides: carousels for saves, numbered lists, contrarian corrections, dark minimal design. These are vendor blogs [Soft]; they match the Social Insider benchmark (carousels lead engagement) from doc 01.
- **Candidate Indian pages found in search results (unverified, not endorsed):** `@psychologicalsfact`, `@psychological_.facts_`, `@allindiamotivation`, `@psychology_facts_in_hindi`. They show the psychology-facts and motivation niches exist in Hindi/Hinglish. Follower counts were not extractable.
- **Hinglish vs English:** the only "data" found is marketing blogs claiming Hinglish drives more engagement (for example a 34% lower drop-off claim for beauty/lifestyle hooks, no primary source). **Still a hypothesis.** Keep the A/B test in the pilot: same post in Hinglish vs English captions on Page A.
