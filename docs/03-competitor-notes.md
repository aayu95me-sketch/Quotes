# Competitor & Niche Notes (Round 2)

Date: 2026-10-20. **Limitation, stated up front:** I cannot browse Instagram or tracking tools (Modash, Social Blade) from this environment. What follows is from search snippets and blog posts. It tells us *what patterns exist*, not verified follower or engagement numbers. The owner's own phone is the best research tool for the next step, see section 5.

---

## 1. What exists

**Global faceless text pages**
- Quote/mental-health giants exist: one revenue ranking lists a 23M-follower quote page earning an estimated $80K–$180K from brand deals and affiliates [Soft, promotional site]. Stoicism pages exist at ~500K that reportedly grew in under two years [Soft].
- Takeaway [My judgement]: the format is proven and big, **but the generic quote space is crowded**. Winning pages pick a *specific audience and angle*, not "motivation".

**India**
- India has big motivation, self-improvement and career creators (hundreds of thousands to millions), mostly face-led (directory snapshots only) [Soft].
- Small Hindi/Hinglish "psychology facts" pages exist (one at ~10K followers; undated) [Soft]. That suggests the niche is **real but not locked up**.
- One 2025 report claims Hinglish captions beat English by ~25% on engagement, with no primary source. **Treat as a hypothesis to test, not a fact.**

## 2. Formats and hooks reported to work [Soft, vendor blogs]

- 7–10 slide carousels with a strong hook slide and a useful, save-worthy body.
- Numbered lists ("7 signs…", "5 habits…"), contrarian takes and corrections ("Stop doing X"), warnings, timelines.
- Dark, minimal, high-contrast text designs (the "marble statue" stoic look).
- **Nothing here is verified by data.** The pilot will produce our own.

## 3. Gaps I can exploit [My judgement]

1. **Specificity beats breadth:** "Discipline for Indian students/young professionals" is a narrower promise than "motivation quotes".
2. **Insight over quotes:** many pages post lifted quotes. Pages that post *original, concrete, slightly contrarian observations* are more shareable and safer under the originality rules.
3. **Save-first design:** end every carousel with something worth saving (a checklist, a rule, a script).
4. **Bilingual advantage:** a page that is good in Hinglish *and* English can serve both audiences (but keep them as separate pages, see briefs).

## 4. Risks in this niche

- Saturation in generic stoic/motivation quote content.
- Mental-health topics: no diagnosis or treatment claims; add a "not a substitute for professional help" line where relevant.
- "Dark psychology" and manipulation content tends to draw policy flags and low-quality audiences. Avoid.

## 5. What the owner can do in 30 minutes (high value)

On your phone, for each of the two niches in doc 04, save 10 accounts that you think are doing well. For each, note: the handle, the follower count, and which 3 posts look most popular (likes/saves/comments vs follower count). Paste it into a chat message or a file in `research/`. This gives me real data I can't get myself.
