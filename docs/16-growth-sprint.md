# 14-day growth sprint (2 to 16 Nov 2026)

Why: day 12 data (n small): Reels reach about 90-160 accounts, almost all non-followers; carousels reach about 3; follows from that reach are close to zero (4 and 5 followers). So the bottleneck is **turning viewers into followers**, then **retention**. The numbers below are assumptions to test, not facts.

## What changes
1. **Hook-first Reel test (v2).** The 6 Reels of 4-9 Nov get a v2 draft: hook promises a result in 6-8 words, 2.6 s per slide instead of 3.2, 3 body slides, last slide an explicit "Follow @handle" line. Hypothesis: shorter Reels with a stronger first second raise watch time and follows. Test: v2 vs the original format on the same topics, 3 Reels each, compared on non-follower reach, saves+shares per 1,000 reach and follows. No conclusion before 8 Reels per format.
2. **Profile conversion.** New bio and 2 pinned posts per account (operator Prompt 6, docs/11). Hypothesis: a clear promise and a pinned proof post lift follows per profile visit.
3. **Safer posting.** Duplicate-safe publisher, max 1 post per account per hour, daily cap 3. Keeps the accounts out of spam filters, which is a precondition for any growth.
4. **Owner 10-minute daily routine (phone):** reply to every comment within a few hours; leave 5 genuine, specific comments on posts in the niche (by hand, no bots, no copy-paste); follow 10 relevant accounts by hand. Early engagement is the cheapest reach we can buy.

## What we measure (daily report, Sunday summary)
Per account: followers (daily), non-follower reach per Reel, saves+shares per 1,000 reach, follows per 1,000 non-follower reach, share of Reels above 3x median. Gate on 16 Nov: if v2 is not better on at least two of the three metrics with 6+ Reels per format, drop it; if follows per 1,000 reach is still under 1, rethink the niche or language mix before adding volume.

## What we do not do
No bots, no follow/unfollow, no buying followers, no new accounts without the owner's decision, no invented claims.
