# Writing style: easy to read (set 2026-10-21 after owner feedback on the first post)

Feedback: the first post (Roz Ka Rule, procrastination) felt a little confusing. Cause: slides 2 to 4 were abstract and up to 17 words ("Aap kaam isliye taalte ho kyunki kaam se judi feeling se bach rahe ho").

Rules for every slide from now on:
1. **One idea per slide, max 10 words** on body slides (hooks max 8). If it needs a comma and an "and", split it.
2. **Concrete before abstract.** Use an action or an example ("Table par padho. Bed par nahi.") instead of an explanation of why.
3. **Plain words.** Hinglish uses everyday spoken words; no long chains of "ka/ki/se/ko" in one sentence. English uses short words.
4. **Highlight the key words** with `*asterisks*` (rendered bold in the page's accent colour). 1 to 3 highlights per slide.
5. **Numbers and labels** help scanning: "*1.* ...", "*Minute 2:* ...", "*Missing half 1:* ...".
6. **Hook = the benefit or the question**, not a statement that needs decoding.
7. **Last slide:** one clear action ("Save karo...") plus the follow line.
8. Type is larger (hook 96/84 px, body 88/76/64 px), so shorter text also fits better on phones.
