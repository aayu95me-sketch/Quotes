# Market Research: Faceless Automated Text/Image Social Page

Research date: 2026-10-20. Status: **Round 1 (desk research).**

## Evidence quality: read first

Almost everything below comes from secondary sources: creator blogs, vendor sites and course sellers. I could not reach Meta's, TikTok's or YouTube's official policy pages from this environment (`facebook.com` did not resolve). So:

- **[Solid]** means Meta/X documentation or a benchmark dataset, as relayed by search.
- **[Soft]** means a blog or vendor claim, often with a conflict of interest, such as a course seller's income claim.
- **[My judgement]** means my inference, not data.

Every threshold or payout number must be re-verified on the official page before we depend on it. Policy in this space changed several times in 2025–26 and will change again.

---

## 1. Objective restated

Maximise money from a faceless, Claude-run page that posts text-based images (carousels and single images) and static-image reels with trending audio. The aim is organic virality, then monetisation.

The most important finding, which shapes everything else:

> **Platform payouts are the small part of the income. Money comes from owning the audience and selling to it.**

Sources put Reels bonuses at roughly 2–5% of income for established faceless accounts, with brand deals and affiliate income making up most of the rest [Soft]. Plan for audience monetisation first, with platform payouts as a bonus.

## 2. Platform-by-platform

| Platform | Fit for static text/image + audio | Direct payout potential | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Best. Carousels are the top-performing format. | Low (bonuses), but best for brand deals, affiliate and funnels | **Primary** |
| **Facebook** | Good. Mirrors IG content. Content Monetization (CMP) reportedly covers photo and text posts [Soft]. | Medium to high only with a US/Tier-1 audience | **Secondary, same assets** |
| **Threads** | Text-native. Free reach test bed. | No active creator bonus found; revenue-share claim unverified | Traffic and idea testing |
| **X** | Text-native | Creator Revenue Sharing retired Sept 2026. Replaced by "Original Content Rewards", paid on verified Home Timeline impressions of original posts. Needs Premium, 500 verified followers and 500k verified impressions in 90 days [Soft, conflicting sources] | Optional, later |
| **TikTok** | Weak. Photo mode is excluded from Creator Rewards, which pays only on original videos longer than 1 minute. Only a few countries are eligible, and India is not on the lists seen [Soft]. | Near zero for this format | Distribution only |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Weak. Static slideshows risk the "inauthentic / repetitive content" classification (July 2026 guidance, [Soft]). | Shorts RPM about $0.02–$1.48 per 1,000 views, versus long-form (AIR, 274 channels) | Skip for now |

### Instagram: key facts

- **Format performance** (Social Insider Q2 2026 benchmarks, brand accounts, so directional for us) [Solid]:
  - Carousels have the highest engagement rate (0.50%) and drive the most saves.
  - Images are the weakest (0.33%).
  - Emplifi's median interactions per post: carousels 46, Reels 39.
  - [My judgement] Lead with carousels for saves and shares. Use static-image Reels for reach and discovery, where audio and the Reels feed give non-follower distribution.
- **Originality gate:** Instagram and Meta are deprioritising unoriginal content. One source reports that accounts posting 10+ reposts in a rolling 30 days are excluded from Explore and Reels recommendations [Soft]. Meta's partner policy says unoriginal content without meaningful enhancement can't be monetised [Soft, relayed]. **Aggregated or lightly-reworded quote content is exactly the high-risk category.**
- **AI content:** Allowed if original. Sources say AI-generated content should carry Meta's AI label [Soft]. The text is generated by Claude and the images are rendered programmatically, so plan to disclose where required.
- **API publishing** [Solid]: the Graph API allows 100 API-published posts per rolling 24 hours, and a carousel counts as one post. Check `GET /<IG_ID>/content_publishing_limit` live, because older docs say 25 or 50. We are nowhere near this limit.
- **Trending audio via API:** Meta has an Audio API that returns trending original sounds or music [Solid]. Caveats:
  - One vendor says accounts connected via "Instagram Login" can't use audio search or attach audio, and that only audio authorised for third-party publishing is returned.
  - The final Reel can't be previewed with audio via the API.
  - **Needs a spike test on a real account.**

### Music and copyright

- Business accounts are reported to get a smaller licensed catalogue than personal accounts [Soft].
- Promotional use counts as commercial. Boosting a post can get trending audio muted or the boost rejected [Soft].
- [My judgement] Trending audio is fine for organic, non-promotional posts. Once a page is clearly commercial (product links, affiliate CTAs), expect friction. Keep a fallback of commercially-licensed or original audio.

### Facebook CMP: key facts

- Eligibility figures **conflict across sources**: roughly 5k–10k Page followers, 60,000 watch minutes in 60 days, 5 active videos [Soft]. It was invite-only in the 2024 beta, and thresholds have been adjusted several times.
- Payout per 1,000 views is **heavily country-dependent**. Estimates vary wildly by source (US Reels about $0.20–$1.50 on one calculator versus $0.03–$0.10 on another; India and SEA near zero on both) [Soft]. [My judgement] The shared conclusion is that US/Tier-1 audiences pay many times more than India/SEA.

## 3. Where the money actually is (ranked, [My judgement] on [Soft] data)

1. **Affiliate and digital products.** Examples: ebooks, templates, planners, prompt packs, and a low-ticket product sold to the page's own audience. Typical affiliate commissions run 10–50% on courses and tools.
2. **Sponsored posts and shoutouts.** Sources quote $50–$500+ per post, depending on size and engagement, and £50–£100 per story shoutout on a 200k page. Theme pages often start selling at around 10k followers, and some start at 1k engaged followers.
3. **Lead generation and funnels.** Move followers to an email list, WhatsApp channel or Telegram channel. This is the only asset that isn't rented from a platform.
4. **Platform payouts.** Treat as 2–5% of income.
5. **Selling the page.** Reported at 3–12x monthly earnings [Soft].

Revenue-per-follower is highest in finance, business and self-improvement niches, and lowest in generic aesthetic/quote niches [Soft, from sites that sell courses]. The reason is simple: the advertisers and affiliate offers pay more.

## 4. Niche selection

The user asked for the niche to be chosen by "fastest path to money". No source I found ranks quote-page niches by saves or shares, so this ranking is **my judgement**, built on monetisation ceiling, content repeatability and policy risk.

| Rank | Niche | Reach potential | Monetisation ceiling | Policy risk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Money mindset / personal finance / wealth habits** | High | Highest (finance affiliates, tools, lead gen) | Medium-high: financial claims, regulated advice | Strong saves/shares. Needs strict "not financial advice" rules. |
| 2 | **Self-improvement: discipline, stoicism, habits, psychology facts** | Very high | Medium to high (courses, books, apps, planners) | Low | Large evergreen audience. Easy to make original. |
| 3 | **Relationships / dating / social dynamics** | Very high shareability | Medium | Medium | Strong sharing in DMs, which Instagram rewards. |
| 4 | Generic aesthetic / motivational quotes | High | Low | **High**: oversaturated and often lifted from others | Don't lead with this. |

**Working recommendation:** Run a **small portfolio of 2–3 pages** (finance/money mindset, discipline/psychology, one wildcard) for 4–6 weeks, then put effort into the winner. A single page is a coin flip, and the cost of extra pages is low once the pipeline exists.

**Audience geography:** Aim for English-speaking Tier-1 audiences (US, UK, CA, AU). They pay more everywhere in the stack, from CMP to affiliate to sponsors. Write in a neutral international/US English voice.

## 5. What "viral" depends on (mechanics)

[My judgement], consistent with the benchmark data above:

- **Saves and shares** are the signals worth optimising. Write posts people want to save for later or send to someone.
- **Slide 1 hook** decides a carousel. **Last slide** should push a save, share or follow.
- **Consistency and volume:** daily posts are plausible given automation, but volume only works if every post is distinct. Templated sameness is the thing that triggers "unoriginal/repetitive" flags.
- **Original angle:** the lines themselves should be **freshly written by Claude** (original observations, not famous quotes). That keeps copyright clean and meets the originality requirement. Use named-person quotes sparingly, attributed and short.
- **Feedback loop:** pull Insights (saves, shares, reach, follows per post) weekly. Double down on formats and topics that beat the median and drop those that don't.

## 6. Automation pipeline (feasibility)

Claude can do the following:

1. **Ideate and write:** topic bank, hooks, carousel scripts, captions, hashtags. Claude writes original lines.
2. **Render:** HTML/CSS templates turned into PNGs (Chromium + Playwright are preinstalled here). Static-image Reels with ffmpeg: image sequence plus audio, no motion needed.
3. **Publish:** Instagram Graph API (carousels and Reels) or a scheduler. Needs a Professional account, a Meta developer app and tokens.
4. **Analyse:** Insights API → weekly report → adjust the content plan.

**Needs a human:** Meta app review and account setup, payout and tax details, brand deal negotiation, and spot-checks of posts, at least in the early weeks.

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Account flagged as unoriginal / mass-produced / spam | **High** | Original text, varied templates and angles, no reposts, AI labelling where required |
| Policy and payout changes | High | Don't depend on platform payouts. Build an email/WhatsApp list early. |
| Copyright on quotes and audio | Medium | Write original lines. Use trending audio only for organic posts. Keep a licensed-audio fallback. |
| Financial / health claims | Medium | Fixed disclaimer, no specific investment advice, a review rule for sensitive topics |
| Platform ToS on automation or multiple accounts | Medium | Use official APIs only. Don't buy accounts or use bots for engagement. Don't create accounts in bulk. |
| Income-claim bias in sources | Medium | Treat all earnings figures as upper-bound anecdotes. Most pages earn little. |
| Single-platform dependence | Medium | Cross-post to Facebook and Threads. Capture an owned audience. |

**Realistic expectation:** I found no reliable data on how many such pages succeed. Course-seller sources show only winners. Plan for the real possibility that individual pages stall, and design the plan around cheap tests and fast kills.

## 8. Things you may be missing (added)

1. **Your country and payout setup.** This affects which programs you can join (TikTok and CMP are country-gated), tax, and how you get paid. *Not yet known.*
2. **Your budget and time.** Costs: tools, a scheduler, optional audio licences, and possibly Claude API usage.
3. **Legal and tax:** a business entity or sole-proprietor setup, tax on foreign income, and FTC-style ad disclosure (`#ad`) for sponsored posts and affiliate links.
4. **Brand identity:** page name, handle, visual system and voice per niche. Brand consistency drives follows.
5. **Owned-audience asset:** an email list or WhatsApp/Telegram channel, set up before the first viral post, not after.
6. **KPIs and kill criteria** (see PROJECT.md): without them a failing page will drain months.
7. **Competitor teardown:** I haven't yet analysed the top accounts in each niche. This is the next research task.
8. **Pinterest and Lemon8:** not researched. Pinterest suits text/image content and has long-lived traffic. Worth a look as a traffic source for the funnel.
9. **Seasonality and trend sources:** a method for finding trending audio and topics each week.
10. **Compliance with AI labelling rules** in each region you target.
