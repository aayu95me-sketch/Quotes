Does Gumroad Work in India? UPI, Payouts, Fees in Rupees

Yes. Indian buyers can pay by UPI on Gumroad, and you get INR to your bank after a $100 minimum and a 7-day hold. Here's what it costs in rupees.

The quick answer

Yes, Gumroad works in India. Buyers here can pay by UPI, and Gumroad pays you in INR to an Indian bank account.

You can't withdraw to a UPI ID. Payouts need a $100 balance (about ₹9,500), wait 7 days, go out on Tuesdays and take 2 to 7 business days to land.

The fee is 10% + $0.50 a sale, plus card processing. On a ₹499 product you keep about ₹359. On a ₹99 product you keep about ₹10.

Yes, you can sell on Gumroad from India, and your Indian buyers can pay you by UPI at checkout. The catch is on your side of the sale: Gumroad holds your money till you cross a $100 balance, pays it out in rupees to your bank once a week, and takes 10% + $0.50 on every sale, which stings at Indian prices.

I'm Nikhil. I built Getvik, so I'm not neutral here. I'll still give it to you straight, including the places where Gumroad beats us. Everything about Gumroad below comes from its own pricing and help pages, which I checked on 20 October 2026.

Does Gumroad support UPI?

For buyers, yes. Gumroad's getting-paid page lists UPI in India as one of the local payment methods at checkout, next to cards and PayPal. Which options a buyer sees depends on where that buyer is, so someone in Jaipur gets UPI and someone in Dubai probably doesn't.

For you, no. UPI is only how buyers pay. Your earnings sit in your Gumroad balance and leave one way, as a bank transfer in INR on Gumroad's schedule. There's no "withdraw to UPI ID" option, and Gumroad's page says plainly that how buyers pay and how you get paid are two separate things.

If you read somewhere that Gumroad has no UPI at all, that's old information. It's there for buyers now.

Does Gumroad pay out to an Indian bank account?

Yes. India is on Gumroad's bank-payout list with INR as the payout currency, and that's the only way it pays Indian creators. No USD payouts, no Payoneer, Wise, cheque or wire. PayPal payouts exist only for countries where Gumroad can't do bank transfers, and once your account is on bank payouts you can't switch to PayPal.

How the money moves, from Gumroad's own page:

You need a $100 balance before anything is sent. At about ₹95 to the dollar that's roughly ₹9,500 sitting with Gumroad before your first payout.

Every sale waits at least 7 days on the weekly, monthly and quarterly schedules.

Tuesday is the payout day for Indian bank accounts. Each payout covers sales made through the previous Friday (UTC).

Your bank then takes 2 to 7 business days to credit it, longer around bank holidays.

The exchange rate is fixed at the time of sale, not at payout.

Add that up and a sale made today is usually in your bank two to three weeks from now, assuming you're already over $100. That's fine for a side income. It's a problem if you're paying for ads from this week's sales.

Before your first payout there's also an account review. Gumroad says it takes 1 to 3 weeks and typically needs 3 or 4 sales with a balance over $100. Don't buy your own product to get there faster; Gumroad says payment processors treat that as fraud and it can get you banned. Use the test purchase feature instead.

Stripe does the identity checks. Keep your full legal name, a physical address (no PO box), a government photo ID and a proof of address ready. If you sell as a registered business, the bank account has to be in the country where the business is registered.

Daily payouts and instant payouts, the ones with no $100 minimum, are for US creators only.

What does Gumroad cost in rupees?

The headline price is 10% + $0.50 per direct sale, 30% on sales that come through Gumroad's Discover marketplace, and no monthly fee. The part most people miss is on the fees help page: the 10% + $0.50 does not include card processing, which Gumroad lists as 2.9% + $0.30, or PayPal fees. The page doesn't say what processing costs on a UPI sale, so I've used the card numbers below.

At about ₹95 to the dollar (20 October 2026), that fixed $0.50 is about ₹48 and the $0.30 is about ₹29. Here's what that does at the prices Indian creators actually charge:

Does Gumroad handle GST in India?

Gumroad says it has been the merchant of record for all sales since 1 January 2025. It collects and remits sales tax worldwide, and you don't manage any tax settings on the platform. For anyone selling to Europe or the UK, where VAT on digital products is a real headache, that's genuinely useful, and it's something we don't do for you at Getvik.

What stays with you: income tax on the money that reaches your bank, and your own GST registration once your turnover needs one. I'm not a CA. Ask one before you cross a few lakh a year.

When is Gumroad the better choice?

Pick Gumroad if most of your buyers are outside India and pay in dollars. At $15 or $25 a product the fixed fees barely register, Gumroad handles the sales tax, and your buyers already trust the checkout.

Pick it if you want Discover. Thirty percent is steep, but Gumroad's marketplace has years of buyers behind it. Getvik has a Discover page too, at your normal fee, but it's far younger and smaller.

Pick it if you sell memberships that charge the card again every month. Gumroad has recurring billing. Getvik memberships are paid once for 30, 90 or 365 days and renewed by hand, which works for a lot of creators but isn't the same thing.

Where Gumroad is a weak fit: your buyers are Indian, your prices are between ₹99 and ₹999, and you want your money this week rather than in three. That's the gap I built Getvik for.

Common questions

Does Gumroad support UPI?

For buyers, yes. Gumroad lists UPI in India as a local payment method at checkout. For you, no. Earnings are paid to your bank account in INR, and there's no option to withdraw to a UPI ID.

Can I withdraw Gumroad money to PayPal from India?

No. Gumroad only pays by PayPal in countries where it can't do bank payouts, and India has INR bank payouts. Its PayPal Connect checkout feature is also not available to creators in India.

What is Gumroad's minimum payout in India?

$100 in your balance, roughly ₹9,500 at today's rate. India has no higher local minimum. Instant and daily payouts with no minimum are for US creators only.

How much does Gumroad charge on a ₹499 sale?

About ₹97 (10% + $0.50) plus about ₹43 card processing (2.9% + $0.30), so roughly ₹140 of ₹499. You'd keep about ₹359. At ₹95 to the dollar; the fixed fees are what hurt at Indian prices.

Does Gumroad handle GST on my sales?

Gumroad says it's the merchant of record and collects and remits sales tax worldwide, so you don't set up tax on the platform. Income tax on what reaches your bank, and your own GST registration if your turnover needs one, are still your job. Ask a CA.

Is there a Gumroad alternative that pays out by UPI in India?

Getvik, which I built. Buyers pay in INR by UPI or card, and you request a payout once your balance crosses ₹100; it usually completes in 2 to 3 business days. Payouts go to the UPI ID and bank account in your name.

Gumroad help: Getting paid by Gumroad

UPI for buyers in India; India listed for INR bank payouts; $100 minimum; 7-day hold; Tuesday payout day for Indian banks; 2 to 7 business days; exchange rate fixed at the time of sale; no Payoneer, Wise or wire; account review of 1 to 3 weeks; daily and instant payouts for US creators only.

Gumroad help: Filling out payout settings
