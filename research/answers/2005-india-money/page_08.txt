How to Sign Up for Amazon Associates India: Step-by-Step

The Amazon Associates India signup process takes approximately 15-20 minutes. Here is the exact process: Step 1, Visit affiliate.amazon.in: Go to the official Amazon affiliate portal for India.

Do not use the .com version, that is the US program and has different commission rates and different eligibility. Step 2, Sign in or create an Amazon account: Use your existing Amazon India shopping account or create a new one.

Your account will be associated with this Amazon India account for all affiliate payments. Step 3, Fill out your account information: Enter your name, address, and preferred Associates store ID (this appears in your tracking URLs).

Step 4, Enter your website or app URL: Amazon requires at least one website, YouTube channel, or mobile app where you'll display affiliate links. You can enter a YouTube channel URL (youtube.com/yourchannel) or your Instagram profile URL.

If you don't have a website, a YouTube channel URL is acceptable. Enter a brief description of your content and primary traffic sources.

Step 5, Complete tax information: Enter your PAN card number. This is required for TDS compliance.

If you don't have a PAN card, you can proceed but your commissions will be subject to higher TDS deduction. Step 6, Review and submit: Review your application.

Approval is typically automatic for established channels and websites. New accounts may go through a brief review.

Step 7, The 3-sale requirement: Important, you must make 3 qualifying sales within 180 days of signup to keep your account active. If you don't, Amazon closes your Associates account and you must reapply.

This is the most common reason new affiliates lose their accounts. Plan your first affiliate content immediately after signup to generate those initial 3 sales.

How to Generate Affiliate Links and Maximize Earnings

Technical setup and optimization for Amazon Associates India: Generating affiliate links: Log in to affiliate.amazon.in, search for the product you want to link, click 'Get Link' to generate a tracking URL.

You can also use the 'SiteStripe' toolbar (a browser extension) that adds a 'Get Link' button to every Amazon product page you visit, this is the fastest method.

Short link option: Use amzn.to short links for social media posts where long URLs look unprofessional.

These are available in your Associates dashboard.

Amazon Native Ads: For YouTube channel or website use, Amazon provides native display ads that automatically show relevant products based on your content.

These earn per-click and per-purchase commissions and require embedding code on a website.

Maximizing the 24-hour cookie: Amazon India uses a 24-hour cookie, you earn commission on any purchase made within 24 hours of a customer clicking your link.

This means: promote products right before high-purchase moments (morning content before people shop, content published day before sale events).

For sale events (Great Indian Festival, Prime Day), publish 'best deals today' content on the morning of the sale day to capture same-day purchasers.

Earning from purchases beyond your specific link: When a customer clicks your affiliate link and then buys ANY product during their 24-hour window (not just the one you linked), you earn commission on their entire cart.

This is why driving customers to Amazon India is valuable even if you're linking a low-commission item, they may add high-commission fashion or grocery items to their cart.

Disclosure requirements: Add to every YouTube description, Instagram bio, and website footer: 'As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.' This is legally required in India under ASCI guidelines and Amazon's own policy.

What are the eligibility requirements for the Amazon Associates program in India?

To be eligible for the Amazon Associates program in India, you must be at least 18 years old and own a functional website, mobile app, blog, or social media channel with original content.

Your platform must be publicly accessible and compliant with Amazon's acceptance policies, which prohibit content that is sexually explicit, violent, or promotes illegal activities.

Applicants from both India and the United States can apply, but they must provide valid identification and tax information relevant to their country of residence.

Amazon does not require a minimum traffic threshold, but a professional-looking site with regular updates increases approval chances.

How do I create and manage my Amazon Associates account in India?

Creating an Amazon Associates account in India begins by visiting the Amazon Associates India homepage and clicking 'Join Now for Free'.

You will need to log in with your existing Amazon account or create a new one.

The application requires you to list your primary website or app, describe its content, and enter your payment and tax details, including a PAN card for Indian residents or a W-9 form for US residents.

Once submitted, Amazon typically reviews your application within 24 to 72 hours.

After approval, you can manage your account through 'Amazon Associates Central', where you track earnings, create links, and view performance reports.

This dashboard is accessible globally and supports both English and Hindi interfaces for Indian users.

What are the commission rates and payment structure for Amazon Associates India?

Amazon Associates India uses a variable commission rate structure that changes based on product categories, with rates ranging from 1% for grocery and personal care products to up to 20% for Amazon fashion and certain beauty items.

The program operates on a bounty structure for some services, such as Amazon Prime sign-ups, which offer a fixed flat fee per action.

Payouts are made monthly, but only after you meet the minimum threshold of INR 2,500 for direct deposit or INR 100 for Amazon gift cards in India.

For associates in the United States targeting the Indian market, the payout thresholds are $10 for direct deposit and $10 for gift cards.

How can I generate affiliate links for Amazon.in products?

You can generate affiliate links for Amazon.in products using the SiteStripe toolbar, which appears at the top of any Amazon.in page once you are logged into your Associates account.

Simply navigate to the product page you wish to promote, click 'Text' in the SiteStripe to get a short link, or click 'Image' to get an HTML code with the product image.

For mobile apps, you can use the Amazon Associates App for Android and iOS to create links on the go.

It is crucial to ensure your links contain your unique Associate ID (tag-20) to track referrals accurately.

You can also use the Product Linking tool in Associates Central to search for products and create links in bulk, which is useful for large-scale content creators in both India and the US.

What are the common reasons for Amazon Associates India application rejection?

Common reasons for rejection of an Amazon Associates India application include providing incomplete or inaccurate information, such as an invalid phone number or incorrect tax details.

Applications are also denied if the listed website or app has no content, is under construction, or contains copyrighted material without permission.

Amazon may reject sites that are primarily designed to send traffic to other e-commerce sites or that violate its trademark policies.

To avoid rejection, ensure your platform has substantial, original content and that all application fields are filled out correctly.

Both Indian and US applicants must adhere to these standards, and re-application is possible after addressing the issues.

Pro Tips

The 'bounty programs' (Prime, Audible, Amazon Music signups) earn flat fees per signup, these can be more profitable than product commissions for content creators with highly engaged small audiences

Amazon's affiliate links must disclose that you're earning a commission, hiding this violates both Amazon's Terms of Service and ASCI guidelines and can result in account termination

Product links in YouTube video descriptions continue earning commissions for months or years after a video is published, evergreen review content has excellent long-term affiliate ROI

Track your Earnings Per Click (EPC) in the Associates dashboard monthly, products with high EPC are the ones your audience buys most reliably; create more content featuring those product categories

During Amazon Prime Day (July) and Great Indian Festival (October), existing affiliate links earn higher commissions due to promotional rate increases, don't wait to produce sale content
