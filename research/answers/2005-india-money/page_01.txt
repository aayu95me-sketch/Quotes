Yes, you can sell on Gumroad from India. Gumroad accepts UPI from Indian buyers and pays Indian creators straight to an Indian bank account in rupees. Two rules shape the experience: you need a 
$100 balance

What does "selling on Gumroad from India" involve?
 You list a digital product on Gumroad, buyers anywhere pay by card, PayPal or (in India) UPI, the money collects in your Gumroad balance, and Gumroad pays it out to your Indian bank account in rupees on its weekly schedule once you cross the minimum.

Key takeaways

Gumroad works in India.
 Its payout article lists India with payout currency INR, and says it accepts "local methods such as UPI in India" at checkout.

Many answers online are out of date.
 Several guides, and Google's own AI summary on 20 October 2026, still say Indian creators get paid only through PayPal and that Gumroad has no UPI. Gumroad's help centre says the opposite on both.

Fees on a direct sale:
 10% + $0.50 plus sales tax. Card processing (2.9% + $0.30) and PayPal fees are extra. Sales through Gumroad's Discover marketplace cost a flat 30% with processing included.

Getting paid takes a while.
 A $100 minimum, a 7-day hold on every sale, and payouts to Indian banks go out on Tuesdays. Instant and daily payouts are US-only.

GST is a real question.
 GST law treats a service as an export only when you are paid in convertible foreign exchange, and Gumroad pays Indian banks in rupees. Ask your CA before you choose.

How to sell on Gumroad from India, step by step

Add a product.
 Upload the file (PDF, video, template, preset) and write the product page. Set your price.

Fill in payout settings.
 Add your Indian bank account. Gumroad's payment processor, Stripe, will ask for identity checks once your sales build up. Have a government-issued photo ID and proof of residence ready. If you sell as a business, the bank account must be in the country where the business is registered.

Share your product link.
 Put it in your Instagram bio, a YouTube description or a newsletter. (If you are still choosing between selling on a platform and building your own site, see

Wait for the minimum.
 Your sales sit in your Gumroad balance until it reaches $100 and the weekly payout runs. More on that below.

One warning before you start. Gumroad says unpaid amounts on a country-specific account "cannot move to a new country's account", so changing your payout country later "forfeits those amounts". Set up the Indian bank account right the first time.

How long until your first payout?

Two rules decide it: the 
$100 minimum
 and the 
7-day hold
.

At $10 a sale, you keep $7.91 per card sale, so you need 
13 sales
 ($102.83) before your balance clears $100 
(calculated)
. At $50 a sale you need 3. A new creator selling a low-priced product can wait weeks for the first rupee.

Once you are over $100, the timing works like this. Each sale waits at least 7 days. The weekly payout covers sales made through the previous Friday (UTC). Indian bank payouts go out on Tuesday, and then the bank transfer can take "a few business days". So even your earliest eligible sale reaches your account more than a week after the buyer paid.

That is the trade-off of a global platform. Gumroad is built to pay creators in many countries on one weekly schedule, and Indian accounts sit inside that schedule.

Gumroad, GST and the rupee payout

This section explains the rule. It is not tax advice, and Gumroad's own tax article says the same about itself.

Since 
1 January 2025
, Gumroad has been the merchant of record for sales on its platform. Its pricing page says it handles "sales tax collection and remittance worldwide", so you do not collect VAT or GST from buyers yourself.

Your own GST position is a separate question. India's GST law zero-rates an "export of services", and the 
GST Council's exports booklet
 states that the definition "requires that the payment for such services should have been received by the supplier of services in convertible foreign exchange". (The Act itself also allows Indian rupees "wherever permitted by the Reserve Bank of India".)

Gumroad pays Indian bank accounts in rupees. Whether your Gumroad income still counts as an export once you are GST-registered depends on how that rupee payout is treated, and that is a question for a chartered accountant 
before
 you cross the registration threshold, not after.

Is Gumroad safe in India?

Gumroad runs payments through Stripe, which carries out identity (KYC) checks on every account that sells, and Gumroad acts as merchant of record for tax. Buyers in India pay through methods they already use, including UPI.

The risks a seller should know are written into Gumroad's own help pages:

Account review holds.
 If an account is under review, for suspected fraud or misleading marketing for example, the balance "stays held until that is resolved".

Payout-country changes can forfeit money.
 Unpaid amounts on a country-specific account cannot move to a new country's account.

Rejected verification.
 If Stripe rejects an account, verification stops and any remaining balance of $1 or more is paid out.

None of this is unusual for a global payments platform. It is worth knowing before you put your income through it.

FAQ

Can we use Gumroad in India?
+

Yes. Gumroad lets Indian creators sign up, list digital products and get paid to an Indian bank account in rupees. Its payout article lists India with payout currency INR, and its checkout accepts UPI from Indian buyers alongside cards and PayPal. Selling is free to start, with fees taken per sale.

How does Gumroad pay you in India?
+

By bank transfer in Indian rupees. Sales collect in your Gumroad balance, wait at least 7 days, and go out in the weekly payout once you have $100. Payouts to Indian bank accounts are sent on Tuesdays and can take a few business days to land. Currency is converted at the rate on the day of each sale.

What is the minimum payout on Gumroad?
+

$100 USD for weekly, monthly and quarterly payouts. Gumroad holds your balance until it reaches that amount. Instant payouts have no $100 minimum, but Gumroad offers them only to creators in the US. At $10 a product sold by card, you need 13 sales to clear $100.

Does Gumroad accept UPI?
+

Yes. Gumroad's payout article says it accepts "local methods such as UPI in India" at checkout, and that the methods a buyer sees depend on where the buyer is. Gumroad does not publish a separate processing rate for UPI payments.

How much does Gumroad charge per sale?
+

10% + $0.50 per direct sale plus sales tax, and Gumroad says this does not include card processing (2.9% + $0.30) or PayPal fees. Sales from its Discover marketplace cost 30% including processing. After $20,000 of sales in a month, new direct sales that month cost 5% + $0.50.

Do I need GST to sell on Gumroad from India?
+

Gumroad collects tax from buyers as merchant of record, but your own GST registration is separate and depends on your turnover. GST law treats a service as an export only when you are paid in convertible foreign exchange, and Gumroad pays Indian banks in rupees. Ask a chartered accountant how this applies to you.

Is Gumroad free to sell on?
+

There is no monthly fee or sign-up charge. Gumroad earns by taking a share of each sale: 10% + $0.50 on direct sales, plus card processing, or 30% on Discover sales. On Peerseek there is also no monthly fee, and the 5% per sale already includes processing.
