Step-by-Step: How to Register for Amazon Associates India

Step 1: Prepare Your Website or Blog

Amazon requires you to have a website, blog, YouTube channel, or mobile app. You need at least 5-10 quality posts or videos before applying. Here is what works best:

Blog:
 Create a niche blog with product reviews, buying guides, or comparison articles. Use affordable hosting like 
Hostinger
 to set up a WordPress site for under ₹150/month.

YouTube:
 Product review or unboxing channels work well. You need some existing videos but no minimum subscriber count.

Social Media:
 Instagram and Facebook pages can also qualify, though websites convert better.

Step 2: Go to Amazon Associates India

Visit the Amazon Associates India portal (
affiliate-program.amazon.in
) and click “Join Now for Free.” You will need:

An 
Amazon.in
 customer account (create one if you do not have it)

Your website URL or YouTube channel link

PAN card number

Bank account details for payment

Mobile number for OTP verification

Step 3: Fill in Your Profile

Payee name:
 Must match your PAN card exactly

Address:
 Your current address (must match bank records for payment)

Website list:
 Add all websites and social media profiles where you will share affiliate links

Preferred Associates Store ID:
 Choose something related to your niche (example: techreviewsindia-21)

Step 4: Describe Your Traffic and Monetization

Amazon asks how you drive traffic and how you plan to use affiliate links. Be honest and detailed:

Select your website’s primary topic categories

Describe your traffic sources (SEO, social media, email list)

Estimate your monthly traffic (be conservative — Amazon will check)

Explain how you plan to integrate affiliate links (product reviews, comparison tables, etc.)

Step 5: Tax Information

Enter your PAN card number. Amazon deducts TDS at 5% on all affiliate earnings above ₹15,000 per financial year. If you have GST registration, add your GSTIN as well — Amazon will include GST on their invoice to you, which you can claim as input tax credit.

Step 6: Verification and Approval

Amazon gives you a provisional approval immediately. However, you must generate at least 3 qualifying sales within 180 days of signing up. If you do not meet this threshold, your account is automatically closed (you can re-apply later).

Method 1: SiteStripe Bar (Easiest)

After logging into your Associates account, visit any product on 
Amazon.in
. You will see a SiteStripe toolbar at the top of the page. Click “Text” to generate a short affiliate link. Copy and paste it into your blog post.

Method 2: Associates Central Dashboard

Log into your Associates dashboard, go to Product Linking and search for any product. You can create text links, image links, or text+image widgets.

Method 3: Amazon API (Advanced)

If you are a developer or use WordPress plugins like AAWP or AzonPress, you can use the Product Advertising API to automatically generate affiliate links and pull product data, prices, and images.

Tips to Maximize Amazon Affiliate Earnings in India

Write comparison and “best of” articles
— “Best Water Purifier Under ₹15,000” converts 3x better than single product reviews.
Target buying keywords
— “buy iPhone 16 online India” converts better than "iPhone 16 features."
Use comparison tables
— visual tables with specifications and affiliate links dramatically improve click-through rates.
Leverage festive seasons
— Amazon Great Indian Festival, Prime Day, and Republic Day sales drive massive purchasing. Prepare your content 2-3 weeks before these events.
Disclose affiliate relationships
— add a disclosure statement on every page with affiliate links. This is legally required and builds reader trust.
Track your links
— use Amazon’s tracking IDs to identify which pages and links generate the most sales. Double down on what works.

Payment Setup for Amazon Associates India

Amazon Associates India pays directly to your Indian bank account via NEFT/RTGS. Here is what you need to know:

Minimum payout:
 ₹1,000 (gift card), ₹2,500 (bank transfer), ₹2,500 (cheque)

Payment schedule:
 60 days after the end of the month (e.g., January earnings paid in early April)

Payment method:
 Direct bank transfer (NEFT) is the most convenient

TDS:
 5% deducted at source on earnings above ₹15,000/year
The 60-day delay is the biggest pain point for new affiliates. Your first payment might come 3-4 months after you start. This is normal — Amazon needs time to process returns and verify purchases. Once the pipeline is rolling, you get consistent monthly payments.

Tax Implications for Amazon Affiliate Income

Amazon affiliate income is taxable as “Income from Business or Profession”

Declare it in your ITR-3 or ITR-4 filing

Amazon deducts 5% TDS — claim credit for this in your ITR

If your total freelance + affiliate income exceeds ₹20 lakh annually, register for GST

Website hosting, domain, and tool expenses are deductible business expenses
Amazon Associates India is not a get-rich-quick scheme. It takes 6-12 months of consistent content creation to see meaningful income. But once your articles rank on Google and the traffic flows, affiliate income becomes genuinely passive — I still earn from articles I wrote two years ago.
Start with one niche, publish 2-3 well-researched product articles per week, and give it six months. The compounding effect of SEO traffic plus Amazon’s trusted brand makes this one of the most reliable online income streams for Indians in 2026.
