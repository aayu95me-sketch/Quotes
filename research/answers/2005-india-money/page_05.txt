Topmate Alternatives in India: Fees and Features Compared

Topmate made it simple for Indian creators to put a price on their time: one link, a booking page and a payment, with no subscription. It is a good product, and for many people it is still the right one. But as creators grow, the same few questions come up. Is a 20% cut on marketplace sales worth it? What if I want to run courses, workshops or a community too? And how do new people find me if I am not already famous on Instagram or LinkedIn?

This guide compares the main alternatives available in India, with the fees each one publishes. We run one of them, The Creator Town, so we have tried to be fair and to say plainly where another tool is the better fit.

What to Compare Before You Switch

The headline commission is only one part of what you pay. Look at five things together:

Total cost at your volume.
 A 0% fee with a monthly subscription can cost more than a 10% fee if you only sell a few sessions a month. Work it out with your real numbers.

What you can sell.
 1-on-1 calls, group workshops, recorded courses and digital downloads are different products. Moving later is painful, so pick a platform that covers where you want to be in a year.

Who brings the buyers.
 Most link-in-bio tools only sell to people you send to them. If you do not have an audience yet, a platform with real discovery matters more than a lower fee.

How payment and no-shows work.
 When are you paid, and what happens if a buyer does not turn up, or you cannot make it?

Who owns the relationship.
 Can you message buyers after the sale, see their history, and sell to them again?

The Alternatives, One by One

SuperProfile

SuperProfile is the closest like-for-like swap. It covers the same ground as Topmate (a profile page with bookings, digital products and courses) and is built for Indian creators. Its main pitch is a 0% platform fee in exchange for a monthly subscription on the paid plan. It suits creators who already sell steadily from their own audience, where a flat subscription costs less than a percentage.

Exly

Exly is a fuller business tool. Alongside consultations and courses it adds a CRM, WhatsApp and email automation, and on higher plans a branded website and community. Its commission drops as the plan price rises, from 10% on the free plan to 3% on Premium. It suits creators who run a real business with repeat customers and want marketing automation in the same place.

Graphy

Graphy is built around courses: a learning platform with live classes, webinars and your own app. It is a strong choice if your main product is a structured course rather than your time, and you are ready to pay an annual plan. It is less of a fit if you mainly sell 1-on-1 calls.

Do It Yourself

A calendar booking tool, a payment link from a gateway such as Razorpay, and a video call app will cost you only the gateway charges. It works well if you have a steady audience and do not mind handling reminders, refunds and invoices yourself. What you give up is any discovery, and the protection a platform gives both sides when something goes wrong.
