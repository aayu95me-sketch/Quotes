Best Topmate Alternatives in India (2026): Fees, Features & Who Each Is For

Akshit Ahuja

 9

Looking for a Topmate alternative in India? The strongest options in 2026 are 
Peerseek, SuperProfile, TagMango, and Stan Store
, picked for lower or zero platform fees, native UPI and Razorpay payouts, and the ability to sell wherever your audience already follows you (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, or YouTube). The best one for you depends on what you sell and how your audience pays.

Key takeaways

Topmate charges 14.16% on a rupee sale through your own link
 (10% platform fee plus a 2% transaction fee, with 18% GST on the fee). That is the number most alternatives compete against.

Peerseek has a 0% platform fee
 (only a 5% transaction charge), which makes it the cheapest pick for creators on any platform.

TagMango and Beacons
 let you trade a monthly fee for a lower commission, which pays off once you sell at volume.

Stan Store
 charges no platform commission but bills in USD, so it suits creators with a global audience more than a purely Indian one.

For most creators under 10k followers, pick the platform with the lowest fee and easiest UPI payout, not the one with the most features.

Transparency note: Peerseek is our platform. The fees below are taken from each provider's public pricing pages as of June 2026; always re-check current pricing before deciding.

Why creators look for a Topmate alternative

Topmate is a solid, well-known platform for booking paid 1:1 calls. But Indian creators commonly switch for three reasons:

Fees.
 At 14.16% on your own link, a 1,000 rupee session nets you ₹858.40. Over a year, that adds up.

Selling where their audience already is.
 Many creators want to sell through their bio link and DMs across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, or YouTube, not only a standalone booking page.

What you sell.
 If your core offer is digital products, courses, or memberships rather than calls, a commerce-first platform fits better.

Use these three lenses (fee, selling channel, offer type) as your decision criteria while you read the comparison.

The best Topmate alternatives, reviewed

1. Peerseek, best for creators on any platform who want the lowest fee

Peerseek is built for Indian creators who want to monetize their audience without a website or coding, whether that audience is on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, or YouTube. It covers paid 1:1 consultations, webinars and cohorts (up to 250 people), digital products, and link-in-bio selling. For creators active on Instagram, it adds Auto-DM that replies to comments with your link automatically. 
Verdict: the cheapest way to start, since the 0% platform fee means you keep more of every sale, no matter where your followers are.

2. SuperProfile, best all-in-one India commerce

SuperProfile gives you a mobile creator page with digital products, courses, paid 1:1 calls, memberships, and a tip jar. It is free to start, with a Premium plan around 11,999 rupees per year and a 5% platform fee. 
Verdict: a strong, feature-rich choice if you sell several offer types at once.

3. TagMango, best for courses and communities at scale

TagMango runs on commission with optional monthly plans that cut your rate: Basic is free at 10%, Pro is 5,000 rupees per month at 5%, and higher tiers drop to 2.5% or lower. 
Verdict: the math works best once you sell enough to justify a paid plan; ideal for course and community creators.

4. Stan Store, best for global audiences

Stan Store uses flat monthly pricing ($29 or $99) with no platform commission, though Stripe and PayPal still take their cut. It is USD-first, so it suits creators selling worldwide more than those collecting mostly UPI payments. 
Verdict: great fee model, weaker fit for a purely Indian, UPI-paying audience.

5. Beacons, best link-in-bio plus store combo

Beacons bundles a link-in-bio page with a store. Its free plan carries a 9% sales fee, while paid plans ($30 and $90) drop that to 0%. 
Verdict: handy if you want your bio link and shop in one place and can grow into a paid plan.

6. Gumroad, best for simple one-off digital downloads

Gumroad is the simplest way to sell a PDF, template, or ebook. It charges 10% on sales from your own page (and a steep 30% on marketplace discovery sales). 
Verdict: fine for occasional downloads, expensive as a primary storefront.

7. Linktree, best for a bio link that works across every platform

Linktree is the most widely used link-in-bio tool, and it now sells digital products too. Commerce fees are tiered: 12% on the free plan, 9% on Starter ($8/mo) and Pro ($15/mo), and 0% on Premium ($35/mo), all on top of Stripe processing. 
Verdict: convenient if you already point every platform's bio at a Linktree, but the commission is steep until you reach the Premium tier.

How to choose your Topmate alternative

By offer type:
 Calls only, stay near Topmate or Peerseek. Digital products and courses, choose SuperProfile or TagMango. A single download, Gumroad.

By payment:
 Mostly UPI from Indian buyers, prioritise Peerseek, SuperProfile, or TagMango. Global buyers, Stan Store works.

By channel:
 Whether your audience is on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, or YouTube, pick a platform that sells through a single bio link so the same link works everywhere (Peerseek, SuperProfile, Beacons).

By stage:
 Just starting or under 10k followers, pick the lowest fee and simplest setup. Scaling past steady monthly sales, a paid plan that cuts commission (TagMango, Beacons) saves more.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Topmate alternative in India?
+

Peerseek does, with no platform fee, only a flat 5% transaction charge. SuperProfile has a free Starter plan too, but as of August 2026 it can't sell anything on it; selling needs the paid Creator plan at ₹499/month plus 5% + GST. TagMango and Beacons have free tiers at higher commission rates.

Which Topmate alternative has the lowest fees?
+

Peerseek has the lowest platform fee at 0% with a 5% per-transaction charge. High-volume sellers can drop TagMango commission to 2.5% or lower, and Stan Store and Beacons top plans charge no platform commission.

Do these platforms support UPI and Razorpay?
+

Peerseek, SuperProfile, and TagMango support Indian payments including UPI. Stan Store, Beacons, and Gumroad are built around international processors, so they fit global audiences better.

Can I sell to my audience on LinkedIn, X, or Instagram?
+

Yes. Peerseek works for creators on any platform through a single bio link you can share on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, or YouTube, plus Auto-DM for Instagram comments. SuperProfile and Beacons also offer link-in-bio selling across channels.
