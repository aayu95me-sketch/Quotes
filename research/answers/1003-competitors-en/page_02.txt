No face. $200,000 a month.

That is not a headline. That is Earthpix, a travel curation page with 24 million Instagram followers and zero personal branding. No creator biography. No face-to-camera content. No name attached. Just stunning landscape photography and an estimated $100,000–$200,000 per month from travel and outdoor brand partnerships.

This is the faceless Instagram model at scale. The 15 pages ranked below earn an estimated $500 to $200,000 per month. Most are run by a single operator. None of them appear on camera.

What Are the Top Faceless Instagram Pages by Revenue in 2026?

The top faceless Instagram pages by revenue are dominated by travel curation accounts (Earthpix at 24.2M followers and Wonderful Places at 20M) and motivational quote pages (@thegoodquote at 23M). Finance and business pages including @wealth (15M followers) earn the highest per-follower revenue due to premium CPMs from fintech and brokerage advertisers. Estimated monthly revenue across this list ranges from $500 to $200,000+, based on follower count and public sponsorship rate benchmarks.

The rate benchmarks start from two public guides. 
Shopify’s influencer pricing guide
 puts Instagram mega-influencer posts at $10,000 to more than $50,000 each.

InfluenceFlow’s 2026 rate guide
 puts Instagram macro-influencers (1M+ followers) at $25,000 to $100,000+ per post. The finance premium and the per-page revenue figures below are Faceless estimates built on those general ranges, not numbers either guide reports.

Three channels drive revenue for every page here. Finance niche pages command 3–5x the per-follower rate of lifestyle pages because finance advertisers, brokerages, and fintech platforms pay a significant premium for engaged audiences. Travel brand deals trail finance but benefit from high deal frequency. Quote and motivation pages scale on volume.

Revenue estimates below assume 2–3 sponsored posts per month at niche-standard rates, plus affiliate revenue. All figures are estimates. Actual results depend on engagement rate, deal terms, and affiliate program performance.

Which Motivation and Quotes Pages Earn the Most on Instagram?

Motivational quote pages dominate Instagram’s faceless creator landscape by follower count. @thegoodquote (23M followers) is the largest quote-based faceless page, earning an estimated $80K–$180K per month from brand partnerships and affiliate deals. The category monetizes through brand safety, not CPM, since inspirational content attracts wellness, productivity, and lifestyle advertisers who pay a premium for positive brand association.

Motivational and quote pages are the most common faceless Instagram format because the barrier to production is low: design a graphic in Canva, write a quote, schedule the post. The top pages have refined this formula into a system that compounds over years.

@thegoodquote (23M followers, quotes / mental health)

The largest pure quote page on Instagram. Founded by Meggan Roxanne, the account posts daily motivational quotes and mental health-adjacent content to an audience that skews heavily female in the 18–35 bracket. Brand deals are with wellness brands, skincare, and productivity apps. The account crosses into content creator territory — Roxanne has become somewhat publicly known — but the page itself runs as a fully faceless format. No face appears in the content.

Estimated monthly revenue: $80K–$180K from brand deals and affiliate links.

@entrepreneurshipquote (3M followers, entrepreneur / mindset)

Posts daily lessons on money, mindset, and execution. Targeted toward aspiring entrepreneurs. Monetizes heavily through affiliated courses and digital products in addition to brand deals. The business angle commands a higher CPM than generic motivation pages.

TheStoic (500K followers, philosophy / stoicism)

Launched in 2023 and grew to 500K in under two years by targeting the growing stoicism and self-discipline subculture. Smaller but tightly engaged audience. Book affiliates and niche brand deals at lower deal rates but strong click-through.

@motivmastery (1.5M followers, motivation)

Clean aesthetic with consistent visual branding. Posts daily. Monetizes through a mix of deal placements and digital product sales, with a growing email list linked from bio.

How Much Do Travel and Nature Faceless Pages Make?

Travel and nature curation pages are the largest category of faceless Instagram pages by follower count. Earthpix (24.2M followers) and Wonderful Places (20M followers) are both travel photo curation pages earning an estimated $80K–$200K per month from travel brand partnerships. The format works because travel brands, hotels, airlines, and outdoor gear companies have large ad budgets and pay a premium for placement on visually relevant pages.

Travel curation is the clearest example of the faceless page model: find stunning photographs, credit the photographer, build an audience around the aesthetic, then monetize through the brands that want to reach that audience. To build a curation page of your own, see 
how to build a faceless Instagram theme page

Earthpix (@earthpix, 24.2M followers)

One of the first major travel curation accounts. Posts landscape and nature photography from contributors around the world. Has operated as a pioneering faceless account for over a decade. Partners with outdoor gear brands, travel booking platforms, and tourism boards. Estimated monthly revenue: $100K–$200K.

Wonderful Places (20M followers)

Nearly identical model to Earthpix, focused on scenic destinations globally. A portfolio page with no single personality attached. Revenue from travel brand partnerships.

@vacations (6.5M followers)

Mid-tier travel page with broad travel content. More accessible deal rates than the mega accounts but still a strong partner for airlines, hotel groups, and travel apps. Estimated monthly revenue: $30K–$80K.

@travly (212K followers)

Built 212K followers in roughly two years with consistent travel photography curation. At this follower tier, brand deals are infrequent. Most revenue comes from affiliate links to booking platforms. Estimated monthly revenue: $500–$2K. Represents the early-stage of a travel page still compounding toward meaningful deal volume.

What Do Finance and Business Instagram Pages Earn?

Finance theme pages on Instagram earn the highest per-follower revenue of any faceless niche. @wealth (15M followers) earns an estimated $80K–$200K per month because finance advertisers, brokerage platforms, and fintech products pay a CPM premium of 3–5x over lifestyle niches.

The finance niche is the highest CPM category on both YouTube and Instagram because the audience has demonstrated money-adjacent intent. Every follower of @wealth is a potential customer for a brokerage, a budgeting app, a crypto platform, or a financial course.

For scale, 
InfluenceFlow’s 2026 rate guide
 says Instagram creators with 10M+ followers start at $75,000+ per sponsored post across niches. By our estimate, finance pages sit at the top of that range.

@wealth (15M followers, finance / business / tech)

Describes itself as “the leading source for all things business, tech, and news.” Posts infographics, business news summaries, and finance tips. With 15 million followers and a finance audience, deal rates are at the top of the market. Revenue also comes from financial product affiliates, which pay per conversion rather than per impression. Estimated monthly revenue: $80K–$200K.

Millionaire Mentor (2.1M followers, motivation / finance)

Straddles the motivation and finance niches, which gives it deal opportunities in both. Earns from finance affiliate programs — investment platforms, credit cards, and trading apps typically pay $20–$500 per referred account. At 2.1M followers with consistent engagement, that affiliate income compounds meaningfully.

@entrepreneursquote (2M followers, business / AI / tech)

Posts business news and tech coverage in graphic form. The AI and tech angle adds a high-CPM layer to the business audience. SaaS companies and productivity tools advertise here. Estimated monthly revenue: $8K–$20K from deals and SaaS affiliate commissions.

How Do Mindset and Self-Improvement Pages Monetize?

Mindset and self-improvement pages earn through high-ticket course and coaching affiliates rather than CPM-driven brand deals. A single affiliate sale for a $500–$2,000 online course generates $100–$600 in commission. Mindset of Greatness (2.6M followers) earns an estimated $12K–$30K per month by combining occasional brand deals with a consistent affiliate-first monetization model targeting its action-oriented, aspiring-entrepreneur audience.

How Do These Faceless Instagram Pages Actually Make Money?

Faceless Instagram pages monetize through four channels: sponsored posts (primary for pages above 1M followers), affiliate marketing (primary for pages under 1M), digital product sales (common in finance and mindset niches), and newsletter revenue (growing as top pages build off-platform lists). Sponsored post rates scale with follower count. By our estimate, a lifestyle page with 1M followers earns roughly $5K–$10K per post, while a finance page at the same size earns $15K–$30K per post.

Affiliate marketing.
 Pays per click-through, lead, or sale rather than per post. Finance affiliates (brokerage signups, trading platforms) typically pay $20–$500 per conversion. High-ticket course affiliates pay 20–50% commission on products priced $200–$2,000. For smaller pages under 500K followers, affiliate revenue often exceeds brand deal revenue because deal rates are low at smaller scales.

Digital products.
 Some pages sell their own content directly: ebooks, templates, and Notion dashboards. Pages in the entrepreneur and mindset niches are well-positioned here. @entrepreneurshipquote earns from its own course sales alongside deal and affiliate income.

Newsletter revenue.
 The smartest operators build an email list from their Instagram audience to reduce platform dependency. Brand deals in email typically pay more per subscriber than Instagram sponsored posts. This is an emerging revenue layer rather than primary income for most pages on this list.

What Separates Top-Earning Faceless Pages from Average Ones?

The highest-earning faceless Instagram pages share four traits: a high-value niche (finance, travel, or entrepreneurship rather than generic quotes), consistent posting cadence (daily or near-daily), a clean visual aesthetic that brands want their names associated with, and a monetization model built around high-commission affiliates rather than waiting for brand deals alone. Pages that stall at $500–$2K per month typically have generic niche positioning and no affiliate stack.

The difference between @travly ($500–$2K/mo at 212K followers) and @wealth ($80K–$200K/mo at 15M followers) is not just scale. It is niche. Finance pages earn 3–5x per follower what lifestyle pages earn.

Four levers that separate top earners:

Niche premium.
 Pick finance, business, or tech over generic lifestyle. The CPM difference is not marginal. At 1 million followers, a personal finance page earns 3–5x more from a sponsored post than a general quotes page.

Engagement rate.
 Pages with 5%+ engagement command premium rates regardless of follower count. NonStopClassy at 400K achieved 500%+ engagement on a viral post, making it more attractive to luxury brands than a 2M-follower page with 0.5% engagement.

Clean affiliate architecture.
 Pages under 1M followers should treat brand deals as bonus income and build affiliate revenue as their primary income. Finance affiliate programs pay $20–$500 per conversion. At 100K followers with 0.5% click-through on a finance post, that is 500 clicks per post and potentially $5K–$10K in affiliate commissions from a single post.

Off-platform list.
 Pages that add an email list absorb Instagram algorithm changes without revenue disruption. A 50K email list in a finance niche is worth more than 500K Instagram followers if Instagram restricts organic reach.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the top faceless Instagram pages by revenue?

By estimated monthly revenue, Earthpix and @thegoodquote are the highest-earning faceless Instagram pages, each generating an estimated $80K–$200K per month. Both have 20M+ followers and operate as pure curation or content aggregation pages with no identifiable face. Earthpix partners with travel brands; @thegoodquote monetizes through wellness and lifestyle brand deals.

Can a faceless Instagram page with under 100K followers make money?

Yes. Pages under 100K followers earn primarily through affiliate marketing rather than brand deals. A finance or entrepreneurship page with 50K engaged followers and a link to a brokerage signup in bio can generate $1,000–$3,000 per month from affiliate commissions alone before a single brand deal. Niche selection matters more than follower count at this scale.

Which faceless Instagram niche has the highest revenue per follower?

Finance and business pages earn the highest revenue per follower on Instagram. A finance page with 1M followers earns an estimated $15K–$30K per sponsored post, compared to $5K–$10K for a lifestyle or travel page at the same size. Fintech companies, brokerage platforms, and business tools pay a significant premium to reach audiences who have demonstrated financial intent.

How do faceless Instagram pages build their initial following?

Most successful faceless pages built their early following through consistent daily posting in a defined niche, strategic use of hashtags and reposting from similar pages, Reels-first strategies (Instagram’s algorithm heavily favors Reels for discovery), and collaboration with other faceless pages in adjacent niches. The pages in this list all published for 6–18 months before seeing significant monetization.

What format works best for a faceless Instagram page in 2026?

Photo curation and text-graphic formats remain the two most scalable faceless Instagram formats. Travel curation builds large audiences quickly but requires sourcing photographers. Text-graphic quotes need only Canva and a content calendar. Reels adaptations of both formats now drive the majority of new follower growth for pages launched post-2024, as Instagram’s algorithm heavily favors Reels.
