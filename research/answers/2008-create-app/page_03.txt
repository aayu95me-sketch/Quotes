# Register as a Meta Developer

Updated: Sep 19, 2024

Copy for LLM

[View as Markdown](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/register.md)

This document explains how to register as a Meta developer. Once you have registered you will have access to the App Dashboard and all of our products, SDKs, APIs, development tools, and developer documentation.

## Step 1: Start the registration process

While logged into your Facebook account, go to [https://developers.facebook.com/async/registration](https://developers.facebook.com/async/registration).

Alternatively, you can go to the [Social Technologies Dev Center](https://developers.facebook.com/) website and click **Get Started**.

## Step 2: Agree to our Terms and Policies

Click **Next** to agree to our [Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms) and [Developer Policies](https://developers.facebook.com/devpolicy).

## Step 3: Verify your account

We will send a confirmation code to the phone number and email address that you provide in order to confirm that you have access to them. Your number and email will be used for important developer notifications of any changes that may impact to your app.

## Step 4: Select your occupation

Select an occupation that most closely describes what you do for a living.

## Next Steps

Now that you have registered you can use the App Dashboard to [create your first app](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/create-an-app).
