In-stream ads for Live is ending on June 15, 2026. After that date, you won’t be able to access the In-stream ads for Live program or any of its features. Eligible creators currently monetizing with Live ads will still be able to earn with [Facebook Content Monetization](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1049081556813520).

Public reels are eligible for monetization in Facebook Content Monetization as long as it’s published in certain countries and languages.
