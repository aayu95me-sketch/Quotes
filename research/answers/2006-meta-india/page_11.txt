Facebook Monetization Countries List 2026

By 

 SAJID 

 / 

 March 13, 2026 

Facebook has become one of the biggest platforms for content creators to earn money online. Millions of people upload videos, reels, and posts every day hoping to generate income. However, many creators are confused about one important thing: 
Facebook monetization is not available in every country.

If you want to earn money from Facebook, you must live in a country where Facebook monetization features are supported. In this guide, we will explore the 
Facebook monetization countries list, eligibility requirements, available monetization tools, and tips for creators who want to qualify faster.

By the end of this article, you will clearly understand whether your country is eligible and how you can start earning from Facebook.

What Is Facebook Monetization?

Facebook monetization refers to the different ways creators can 
earn money from their content on Facebook
. The platform allows creators to generate income through advertisements, subscriptions, bonuses, and fan support.

Over the last few years, Facebook has introduced several monetization programs to compete with other platforms like YouTube and TikTok.

Some of the most popular Facebook monetization features include:

In-stream ads

Facebook Reels bonus

Fan subscriptions

Stars

Branded content partnerships

Ads on Reels

However, these features are only available in 
approved countries
. If your country is not supported, monetization options may not appear in your professional dashboard.

Why Facebook Monetization Is Limited to Certain Countries

Many creators wonder why Facebook restricts monetization in some regions. The reason mainly involves:

1. Advertising Market

Facebook monetization depends heavily on advertising. Countries with stronger advertising markets are usually supported first.

2. Payment Infrastructure

Facebook needs reliable banking systems to send payments to creators.

3. Policy Compliance

Some countries have legal or regulatory issues that make it difficult for Facebook to operate monetization programs.

4. Content Safety Systems

Facebook prioritizes regions where content moderation and safety systems are easier to manage.

Because of these factors, Facebook gradually expands monetization availability instead of launching it worldwide at once.

How to Check If Your Country Is Eligible

Instead of relying only on lists, the best way to check eligibility is directly from Facebook.

You can verify your country using the 
Facebook Monetization Manager
 or 
Professional Dashboard
.

Follow these steps:

Open your Facebook Page.

Go to 
Professional Dashboard
.

Click 
Monetization
.

Check the eligibility status.

If your country is supported and your page meets the requirements, Facebook will show available monetization options.

Facebook Monetization Requirements

Living in a supported country is not enough. Your page must also meet several eligibility requirements.

Here are the most common requirements.

1. Minimum Followers

Most monetization features require at least:

10,000 followers
 on your Facebook page

2. Watch Time

For in-stream ads, creators usually need:

600,000 minutes of watch time in the last 60 days

3. Active Video Content

You must upload at least:

5 active videos
 on your page

4. Community Standards

Your content must follow Facebook policies such as:

No copyrighted material

No violent or misleading content

No fake engagement

Violating these policies can remove monetization access.

Facebook Monetization Features Available

If your country is supported, you may be able to use several monetization tools.

In-Stream Ads

This feature allows ads to appear inside your videos. You earn a share of the advertising revenue based on views and engagement.

It is one of the most popular monetization methods for long-form Facebook videos.

Facebook Stars

Facebook Stars allow viewers to support creators during live streams or videos.

When viewers send Stars, creators earn money directly from Facebook.

Fan Subscriptions

Fan subscriptions allow followers to pay a monthly fee to access exclusive content.

Creators can offer perks such as:

Exclusive posts

Subscriber-only videos

Special badges

Reels Ads

Short-form videos are growing rapidly, and Facebook now places ads within Reels.

Creators can earn revenue based on the performance of their reels.

Brand Collaborations

Many creators earn money through brand deals.

Businesses pay creators to promote products through Facebook posts or videos.

Tips to Qualify for Facebook Monetization Faster

If you want to get approved for monetization, focusing on the right strategies can make a huge difference.

Create High-Quality Video Content

Videos perform much better than text posts.

Focus on creating:

Engaging videos

Informative content

Entertaining reels

Consistency is extremely important.

Post Regularl

Facebook favors creators who upload content frequently.

Try to upload:

3–5 videos per week

Daily reels if possible

This helps grow followers and watch time faster.

Avoid Copyright Issues

Using copyrighted music or videos can block monetization.

Always use:

Original content

Licensed music

Facebook sound library

Focus on Audience Engagement

Facebook algorithms prioritize engagement.

Encourage viewers to:

Like

Comment

Share your videos

Higher engagement often leads to more reach and monetization opportunities.

Common Reasons Facebook Monetization Gets Rejected

Many creators apply for monetization but get rejected. Some common reasons include:

Posting reused content

Copyright violations

Fake followers

Low engagement

Community guideline violations

Fixing these issues can help you become eligible again.

Future Expansion of Facebook Monetization

Facebook continues expanding monetization to new countries every year.

As the platform grows, more creators around the world will gain access to monetization features.

Experts expect Facebook to introduce:

New creator bonuses

Improved ad revenue programs

Better creator tools

This makes Facebook one of the best platforms for long-term content creators.

Final Thoughts

Facebook monetization has created massive opportunities for creators worldwide. However, eligibility depends on two main factors: 
living in a supported country and meeting Facebook’s monetization requirements.

If your country is on the Facebook monetization countries list, the next step is building a strong page with engaging video content and active followers.With consistency, creativity, and adherence to Facebook policies, creators can turn their Facebook pages into a reliable source of online income.
