FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I cite these statistics in my article, paper, or presentation?

You can use the built-in "Copy APA Citation" button next to any statistic. Each citation includes the official author (ZocialOne Research), publication year (2026), report title, and canonical page URL.

What is the primary data source behind the ZocialOne Statistics Index?

Data is compiled from aggregated, anonymized performance metrics across 1,25,000+ Indian SMB and brand accounts managed via ZocialOne, alongside public benchmark reports from Meta, YouTube, and digital marketing audits across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities in India.

How frequently is this statistical index updated?

The ZocialOne State of Indian Social Media Marketing index is updated quarterly to reflect evolving consumer habits, language preferences, festive engagement spikes, and AI adoption benchmarks across India.

Are these statistics free to use for press, blogs, and academic research?

Yes, all statistics on this page are free to cite for journalists, marketers, researchers, and content creators with appropriate attribution linking to https://www.zocialone.ai/stats/india-social-media-statistics.
