FAQ

How much do Instagram influencers earn in India?

It depends on followers, engagement and niche. As a benchmark, a lifestyle creator charges about ₹1,400 – ₹2,300 per Reel at 10,000 followers, ₹9,000 – ₹15,000 at 100,000 and ₹45,000 – ₹75,000 at 1 million. On InfluencerMetric itself, the median Instagram Reel is listed at ₹2,000 across 80 live packages (counted 20 October 2026); those are asking prices, not settled invoices.

Does Instagram pay creators per view?

Not the way YouTube does. YouTube shares ad revenue with Partner Programme channels; Instagram has no equivalent fixed rate per view, so most creator income comes from brand deals.

How much can I charge with 10K followers?

About ₹1,400 – ₹2,300 for a Reel, ₹800 – ₹1,400 for a static post and ₹500 – ₹800 for a single Story, in a lifestyle niche at typical engagement. Strong engagement or a high-demand niche moves you to the top of the range or above it.

What engagement rate is good on Instagram?

The benchmarks this calculator uses: Nano (1K–10K) 4%, Micro (10K–50K) 2.5%, Mid-tier (50K–500K) 2%, Macro (500K–1M) 1.5%, Mega (1M+) 1%. Engagement well above your tier's figure can raise your price by up to 50%; well below it lowers the estimate.

Which niches pay the most?

Finance and investing (×1.4), tech (×1.3) and education (×1.2) earn the biggest premiums, because their audiences buy higher-value products. Entertainment and comedy trade slightly below the baseline.

How do I get brand deals?

List your services with clear prices so brands can book without back-and-forth. On InfluencerMetric, brands book creators directly and payment is held in escrow until the content is approved.
