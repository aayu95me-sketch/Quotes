Average Influencer Rates in India 2026 (Instagram, YouTube, Reels)

How much influencers charge in India in 2026 — Instagram reel, story, and YouTube rates by follower tier, plus what changes the price. Real INR bands.

Nayan Chaurasia

 29 May 2026

 2
 min read

Key takeaway

In India in 2026, Instagram reel rates run from about ₹1,500 for nano creators to ₹1,50,000+ for macro creators, YouTube integrations cost more, and the final price depends on engagement, niche, usage rights, and exclusivity — not follower count alone.

How much do influencers charge in India? There is no fixed price, but there are reliable bands. The number depends on platform, follower tier, engagement, niche, and — often overlooked — usage rights and exclusivity. Below are realistic 2026 ranges in INR you can use to set or negotiate a rate.

What changes the price

Engagement rate — a 3% creator is worth more than a 0.8% creator at the same size.

Niche — finance, tech, and beauty often command premiums over general lifestyle.

Usage rights — letting the brand run your content as a paid ad adds 20–50%+.

Exclusivity — not posting for competitors for a period costs extra.

Volume — a 5-reel package usually earns a per-unit discount.

Turnaround — rush delivery is a legitimate surcharge.

Engagement beats follower count

A micro creator with high engagement and a tight niche often delivers better cost-per-result than a macro name. Price on value delivered, not vanity metrics.

How to quote a rate confidently

Set a base rate per deliverable, then add line items for usage rights, exclusivity, and rush. Put it in writing. A clear, itemised quote signals professionalism and is far easier for a brand to approve than a single lump sum.

Frequently asked questions

How much do influencers charge per post in India?
+

In 2026, Instagram reel rates run roughly ₹1,500–₹8,000 for nano creators (1K–10K), ₹8,000–₹40,000 for creators up to 100K, and ₹40,000–₹1,50,000+ for larger creators. Stories and static posts cost less; YouTube costs more.

How much do YouTubers charge for brand deals in India?
+

A YouTube integration typically costs ₹15,000–₹70,000 for channels of 10K–100K subscribers and ₹70,000–₹2,50,000 for 100K–500K. Dedicated videos cost significantly more than integrations.

What affects an influencer's rate the most?
+

Engagement rate, niche, usage rights, exclusivity, volume, and turnaround. Two creators of the same size can charge very differently based on engagement and how the brand intends to use the content.
